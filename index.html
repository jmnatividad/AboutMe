01 / About

A developer who thinks beyond the code.

I’m a software developer who enjoys building applications that are practical, reliable, and easy to maintain. Most of my experience has been with Laravel and PHP, JavaScript, databases, APIs, and modern frontend tools. I like understanding not just how something should look, but also how it works behind the scenes and how it should behave for the people using it.

Over the years, I’ve also worked with software testing, Docker, AI-assisted development, automation, Unity and C#, mobile applications, and technical training. Working across these areas has taught me to look at a feature from more than just the coding side — from understanding the requirement and building it, to testing it, fixing problems, and documenting how it works.

I’m always interested in learning new technologies and finding better ways to build software that solves real problems.
